Local RAG notes

The embedding model is BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5. It is small at around 130MB
and produces 384-dimensional vectors. It is a good fit for local retrieval
because it is fast on CPU and strong for its size.

The reranker is cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2. It scores query and
chunk pairs directly, which is more accurate than vector similarity alone,
and it is fast enough to rerank the top twenty candidates on every query.

The generation model is llama3.2:3b served by Ollama. It runs well on
Apple Silicon Macs and is the default because it is the most reliable
small local language model for this setup.

ChromaDB is the vector database. It runs embedded, persists to a local
directory, and needs no server or Docker container. That is what makes the
whole service clone-and-run on a Mac.
