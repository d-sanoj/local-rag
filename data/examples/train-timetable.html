InterCity timetable — Friday departures

All trains leave from the main station. Times are in local time.

Evening sleeper service

The InterCity sleeper service to the north coast departs at 21:40 from platform 7. It calls at three stations overnight and arrives at 07:15 the next morning. Sleeping berths must be booked in advance, and the dining car serves breakfast from 06:30.

Daytime departures

Daytime InterCity trains to the capital leave every hour from platform 3, the first at 06:05 and the last at 19:05. The journey takes two hours and ten minutes with no changes.
